# T-007 Landmark References

OSM and Overture entries were first recorded 2026-11-22. Street View was inspected
2026-11-27–28. Google imagery is reference only: no pixels, logos or photographs are in the GLBs.

Accuracy status: four models in this batch have an inspected January 2026 frontage. Hidden sides
remain plain. The other legacy GLBs pre-date this survey and are not claimed as accurate until a
visible reference can be tied to the mapped footprint.

---

## 1. 535333512 — Salt Lake Sector V Metro Station
- **OSM Way:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/535333512
- **Corridor:** Green Line (East-West Metro Corridor), Kolkata Metro
- **Footprint:** L ≈ 139.15 m, W ≈ 13.95 m, 4 floors (~18.5 m)
- **Architecture:** Elevated viaduct rapid transit station with 7 portal frames, ochre composite cladding, ribbon glazing, barrel-vault canopy roof.

## 2. 193308962 — Wipro Tower 5
- **OSM Way:** https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/193308962
- **Footprint:** 22-point polygon, 61.2 m × 82.6 m, 10 floors (~38 m)
- **Street View:** https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=k8XETmHtdyTDClhRZXwF0g&heading=43.22&pitch=0 (Jan 2026)

  orange-red recessed balcony bays. Gate and trees obscure the ground floor.
- **Status:** the legacy GLB has not yet been rebuilt from this reference and is not part of the
  surveyed four-model batch.
