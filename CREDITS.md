# Vehicle model credits

The traffic cars in `public/models/vehicles/` are simplified conversions (exterior only, about 20k triangles,
recolourable paint) of Sketchfab models from the collection
https://sketchfab.com/K.G.S/collections/indian-cars-41fdaf53624249c98c8de412cb9de03d

They are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY). The original authors must be credited by name
and the licence checked on each model's page before this ships. Authors were not verified automatically
(Sketchfab blocked the lookup); fill in the author column from each model page.
